E-Commerce Platform-Specific Requirements

144|

Each marketplace has different image requirements. Here's what you need to know for the major platforms:

145| 146|
147|

🛒 Amazon

148|

Main image requirement: Pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255). The product must fill at least 85% of the frame. Minimum 1000px on the longest side for zoom functionality.

149|

Pro tip: Amazon strictly enforces the white background rule. Use the white background preset in BG Vanish to ensure you're hitting pure #FFFFFF — even slightly off-white can get your listing suppressed.

150|

Additional images: Secondary images (lifestyle, scale, detail shots) can have non-white backgrounds. Use these to show context and features.

151|
152| 153|
154|

🛍️ eBay

155|

Main image requirement: White or light gray background recommended but not strictly enforced. Minimum 500px on longest side, 1600px recommended. No watermarks, borders, or text on images.

156|

Pro tip: While eBay is more flexible, products with clean white backgrounds consistently outperform cluttered photos in eBay's search results. The 1600px recommendation matters — eBay's zoom feature drives engagement.

157|
158| 159|
160|

🏪 Shopify

161|

Main image requirement: No strict requirements — but 2048×2048px square format is recommended for best results across themes. Background can be white, transparent, or contextual.

162|

Pro tip: Use transparent PNGs for maximum flexibility. Shopify themes handle transparent product images well, and they work seamlessly on both light and dark mode storefronts. BG Vanish outputs high-quality transparent PNGs by default.

163|
164| 165|
166|

🎨 Etsy

167|

Main image requirement: No strict background rules, but clean product photos are highly recommended. Minimum 1000px wide, 3000px ideal for the best user experience. Etsy encourages showing products in context.

168|

Pro tip: Etsy buyers appreciate both studio-style (plain background) and lifestyle shots. A common strategy: use a white-background image as your primary photo for search visibility, and lifestyle images as secondary photos to tell a story.

Product Photography Best Practices

195|

Background removal works best when the original photo is well-shot. Here are tips for getting the most out of AI background removal:

196| 197|
    198|
  • Shoot on a contrasting background — if your product is white, shoot on gray or colored background so the AI can easily distinguish edges
    • 199|
  • Use even lighting — avoid harsh shadows that the AI might interpret as part of the background
    • 200|
  • Shoot at a slight distance — leave some padding around the product. The AI needs to see the edge to remove the background cleanly
    • 201|
  • Avoid motion blur — sharp edges produce the best results. Use a tripod for small products
    • 202|
  • Shoot at the highest resolution — marketplace zoom features reward high-res images. Minimum 2000px for future-proofing
    • 203|
  • Capture multiple angles — front, back, side, detail, and 45-degree views. More images = higher conversion.
    • 204|
