Pro Tips for E-commerce Product Photos

111|

1. Shoot Against a Contrasting Background

112|

AI background removers work best when your product clearly contrasts with the background. Avoid shooting a white product on a white surface — use a contrasting color. The AI doesn't care about the color; it just needs to distinguish subject from background.

113| 114|

2. Good Lighting = Better Results

115|

Even the best AI struggles with grainy, poorly lit photos. Shoot your products in natural daylight or with softbox lighting for crisp edges and accurate color.

116| 117|

3. Add Shadows for Depth

118|

Pure white backgrounds can look flat. After removing the background, add a subtle drop shadow to your product image for a professional, studio-lighting effect. Most e-commerce platforms support shadow overlays or you can add one in any image editor.

119| 120|

4. Maintain Consistent Angles

121|

For multi-product stores, shoot all products from the same angle and distance. This creates a cohesive storefront that builds buyer confidence.

122| 123|

5. Test Mobile Display

124|

Over 60% of e-commerce traffic comes from mobile. Your product should be clearly visible on a 375px-wide phone screen. Crop tightly around the product to maximize screen real estate.

125| 126|

127|
    128|
  • Leaving shadows from the original background: Always verify edges are clean
