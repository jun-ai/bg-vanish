Frequently Asked Questions

What image formats are supported?▼
You can upload PNG, JPG, and WebP images. Maximum file size is 10MB per image. Results are always delivered as high-quality transparent PNG files.
How long does processing take?▼
Most images are processed in 3–5 seconds. Larger or more complex images may take slightly longer, but you'll typically have your result almost instantly.
Is my image data private?▼
Yes. Your images are processed server-side and never stored. We do not keep copies, share them with third parties, or use them for AI training.
How many free credits do I get?▼
New users receive 3 free credits upon signing up with a Google account. Each credit lets you process one image. After that, you can purchase credit packs starting at $4.99 for 40 credits.
Can I process multiple images at once?▼
Yes! With the Pro plan, you can upload and process up to 5 images simultaneously. Each image in the batch consumes one credit.
